Selected work

Acquisition screening for private equity buyers

South Grand Group · Summer 2026

I screened more than 25 acquisition targets against four buy-side mandates, testing business model, revenue quality and ownership structure. I built a 12-field intake framework so the four mandates could be worked from one pipeline, and automated the first-pass screen with Claude Code. When every early candidate for one client fell below its $10M revenue floor, I rebuilt the sourcing screen around that constraint.

Result: more than half disqualified, 8 passed to the buyers for diligence, and the first qualifying target surfaced for that client.

Digital-asset positioning of U.S. banks

Ledger Ridge Partners · Summer 2026

I benchmarked the digital-asset competitive position of 33 U.S. banks with a three-pillar framework, which isolated a settlement gap common to every institution. I synthesized more than 15 primary sources into recommendations and reprioritized the second-tier pipeline around regulatory timing.

Result: 6 recommendations adopted for the firm's Tier 1 client outreach.

Redesigning a graduate hiring program

Korea Commission for Corporate Partnership · 2023–2025

A program meant to help small firms hire university graduates was failing. Interviews with more than 30 companies showed the subsidy was reaching employers rather than the graduates who carried the risk, so I redesigned the program around that finding.

Result: 246 placements across 14 firms, about five times the prior level.

Startup relocation to a former mining region

Korea Commission for Corporate Partnership · 2020–2023

I selected 12 startups to relocate into a former coal-mining region and took ownership of the project despite internal reluctance about the commission taking a direct role.

Result: relocation investment plans totaling more than $27M.

Compliance platform for a manufacturer and its partners

Hyundai Rotem · 2018–2020

I developed the company's compliance strategy as the function was first introduced, audited 10 business units, and launched a centralized platform for roughly 500 partner companies.

Result: document review time down 40% and regulatory breaches down 33%.
